Spoiler-light reading tool

The afterword-boundary card for separating text and commentary

An afterword can supply history, interpretation, translation context, or reception that the main work did not contain. This card keeps later commentary from silently replacing the reading experience.

Build the card

Save one page where the method becomes useful. Complete the fields in your own words.

FieldPrompt
Main-text viewWhat did you conclude before the afterword?
New contextWhat factual or historical information is added?
InterpretationWhat reading does the commentator propose?
BoundaryWhich parts belong to the work and which to later reception?

Run the check

  • Preserve your pre-afterword uncertainty.
  • Separate new evidence from persuasive authority.
  • Return to the cited page before adopting a new interpretation.

Working sentence: My current reading is ______________________________.

Review rule

Write two notes: what the afterword clarified and what remains your own unresolved reading.

Boundaries

This is a personal reflection aid, not a quotation archive, plot substitute, academic citation, factual verification, or proof of an author's intention. Return to the full passage before making consequential claims.
