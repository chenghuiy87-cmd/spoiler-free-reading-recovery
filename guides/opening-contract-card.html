The opening-contract card for a new book

A book's opening teaches the reader what to notice, what kind of uncertainty to tolerate, and how quickly answers may arrive. This card captures that contract without predicting the plot.

Build the card

Save one page where the method becomes useful. Complete the fields in your own words.

FieldPrompt
AttentionWhat does the opening repeatedly direct you toward?
KnowledgeWhat information is deliberately withheld?
PaceHow quickly do scenes, claims, or images change?
PromiseWhat kind of experience seems to be offered?

Run the check

  • Use features visible in the opening pages.
  • Separate genre expectation from this book's actual signals.
  • Keep the promise provisional until a later section confirms it.

Working sentence: My current reading is ______________________________.

Review rule

At one quarter of the book, compare the early contract with the reading experience and record any deliberate break.

Boundaries

This is a personal reflection aid, not a quotation archive, plot substitute, academic citation, factual verification, or proof of an author's intention. Return to the full passage before making consequential claims.
