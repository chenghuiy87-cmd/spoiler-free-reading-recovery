Build a pair of return pages

Two pages can form a small reread path without a full recap.

Choose the page where an action starts.

Choose the page where your reaction shifts.

Write what to compare between them.

Invented example: p.17: an invitation; p.54: the seat still empty; compare the waiting.

TabMap for iPhone and iPad: manually mark the page, choose Keep, Felt, Clue or Discuss, and add an optional private note. One complete book map is free; no app account required.
