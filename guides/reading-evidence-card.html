A fictional reading moment

In the invented novel The Reed House, a visitor waits outside a locked door and leaves without knocking. That is the observation. She is hiding something is one possible inference. A nearby mention of a closed household and a late hour could support another explanation. Return to the preceding exchange before treating a motive as established.

For discussion, keep every reference inside the agreed reading boundary. A private reaction is not a verdict about a character or another reader.
