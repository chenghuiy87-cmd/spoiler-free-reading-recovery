The incentive-incidence card for economics reading

A policy may target one party while costs and benefits travel elsewhere. This card separates the formal rule from behavioral response and final incidence.

Build the card

Save one page where this method becomes useful. Complete the fields in your own words so the page remains the route back to the source.

FieldPrompt
RuleWhat tax, subsidy, price, or constraint changes?
TargetWho is named by the policy?
ResponseHow might behavior or prices adjust?
IncidenceWho ultimately bears cost or receives benefit?

Run the check

  • Separate legal responsibility from economic burden.
  • Name the time horizon of the response.
  • Ask which group has alternatives and bargaining power.

Working sentence: My current reading is ______________________________.

Review rule

Compare the predicted incidence with the evidence actually presented and keep assumptions explicit.

Boundaries

This is a personal reflection aid, not a quotation archive, plot substitute, academic citation, factual verification, or proof of an author's intention. Return to the full passage before making consequential claims.
